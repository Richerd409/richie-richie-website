Built in India's largest knitwear export cluster

Tiruppur ships knitwear to practically every major market on earth, accounting for more than half of India's knitwear exports. But for an individual manufacturer, going from domestic sales to a steady export order book is a maze: certifications, buyer trust, payment terms, freight, and paperwork in unfamiliar formats. We work both sides of that equation — helping Tiruppur businesses reach international buyers, and helping international buyers source confidently from Tiruppur.

What our export service covers

  • Market entry strategy — choosing target markets based on your product, pricing, capacity, and certifications, with realistic entry plans
  • Buyer–seller matching — identifying and approaching genuine buyers, importers, and distributors; verifying counterparties before commitments are made
  • Compliance guidance — documentation, IEC, labelling norms, and the certification expectations of European and other regulated markets
  • Logistics coordination — freight forwarder selection, shipment planning, and keeping your first consignments on schedule
  • Sourcing for overseas buyers — on-the-ground verification of Tiruppur manufacturers, capacity checks, and order follow-up

The honest approach to going global

Export consulting attracts big promises. Ours is deliberately modest: we will tell you if your product is not ready for a market, if a buyer looks doubtful, or if your pricing will not survive freight and duty. Export relationships are built over years, and our role is to make sure the foundation — the first market, the first buyer, the first shipment — is solid enough to build on.

Frequently asked questions

Can you help a first-time exporter start exporting?

Yes. We guide first-time exporters through the essentials — IEC registration, documentation, pricing for export markets, finding genuine overseas buyers, and coordinating the first shipments — so you avoid the costly mistakes new exporters commonly make.

Which markets do you help exporters reach?

We have sourced buyers and leads across Europe, Scandinavia, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia. The right market depends on your product, capacity, and certifications, which we assess before recommending where to focus.

Do you also help overseas buyers source from Tiruppur?

Yes. We match international buyers with verified Tiruppur manufacturers — checking capacity, quality systems, and compliance on the ground before any introduction is made.
