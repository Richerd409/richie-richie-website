Workforce supply for India's knitwear capital

Tiruppur runs on people. As Tamil Nadu's garment and textile export hub, the city's factories, dyeing units, and export houses compete for the same pool of skilled workers — and losing a week of production to unfilled positions costs real money. Our manpower service exists to close that gap quickly and reliably.

We recruit and place candidates across the full spectrum of roles a Tiruppur business needs:

  • Production roles — tailors, power table operators, checkers, ironers, packers, and helpers for knitwear and garment units
  • Supervisory staff — line supervisors, quality controllers, production managers, and merchandisers
  • Office & back-office — accountants, documentation executives, export coordinators, and administrative staff
  • Bulk & seasonal hiring — coordinated drives for peak production seasons and new unit launches

How our recruitment process works

Every placement follows the same disciplined path. We start by understanding the role properly — not just the designation, but the shift patterns, skill level, and temperament your floor actually needs. We then source candidates through our local network, screen them in person, verify references and prior employment, and present you a shortlist worth your time. After placement, we follow up to make sure the fit holds.

Because we are based in Tiruppur ourselves, we know the local labour market — which areas workers commute from, what fair wages look like by skill grade, and how to retain people once hired. That local depth is what separates a staffing partner from a CV-forwarding service.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of manpower do you supply in Tiruppur?

We place skilled and semi-skilled workers for garment and textile units — tailors, checkers, packers, machine operators — as well as supervisors, office staff, accountants, and back-office roles for businesses across Tiruppur and nearby districts.

How fast can you provide candidates?

For common production roles we can usually shortlist vetted candidates within a few working days. Specialised or supervisory roles take longer because every candidate is screened and reference-checked before introduction.

Do you handle bulk hiring for factories?

Yes. We manage bulk and seasonal hiring drives end to end — sourcing, screening, documentation, and onboarding coordination — so production targets are met without burdening your HR team.
