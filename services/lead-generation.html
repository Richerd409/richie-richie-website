Research-first prospecting

Cold lists waste sales time. Our lead generation starts from your ideal customer — the industry, size, geography, and buying signals that define a genuine prospect — and builds outward through manual research. Every company on your list is confirmed active; every contact is verified as the right person; every entry notes why they are worth approaching. Your sales effort starts with the hard filtering already done.

What you receive

  • Ideal customer profiling — a sharp definition of who buys, refined with you before research begins
  • Verified prospect lists — company, decision-maker, role, contact details, and qualification notes; no recycled databases
  • Outreach support — first-contact messaging by email or LinkedIn, handing you only responding, qualified leads
  • Market coverage — Europe and Scandinavia, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and domestic Indian markets
  • Pipeline reporting — clear weekly numbers: researched, contacted, responded, qualified

Built for exporters first

This service grew out of our export consulting work: Tiruppur manufacturers kept asking the same question — "can you find us buyers?" The discipline that finds a knitwear importer in Stockholm works equally well for software firms, logistics providers, and industrial suppliers hunting B2B clients. If your customer is another business, we can build your list.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from buying a database?

Purchased databases are stale and untargeted. We research prospects specifically for your offer — confirming the company is active, the contact person is current, and there is a genuine reason they might buy — so your outreach starts warm instead of cold.

Which regions can you generate leads in?

We have delivered prospect research across Europe and Scandinavia, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia, as well as domestic Indian markets. Language and time-zone handling are part of the service.

Do you also do the outreach, or just provide lists?

Either. Some clients want verified prospect lists to work themselves; others want us to handle first-contact outreach and hand over only responding, qualified leads. We agree the model and the qualification criteria before starting.
