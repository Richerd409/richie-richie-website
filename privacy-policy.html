Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 June 2026

This policy explains what information Richie&Richie Agency collects when you visit richieandrichie.com, how it is used, and the choices you have — including how third-party advertising and cookies work on this site.

Who we are

Richie&Richie Agency (“we”, “us”, “our”) is an enterprise-solutions firm based in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, India, operating the website https://www.richieandrichie.com. For any privacy question you can reach us at the contact details at the end of this page.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — when you contact us through the enquiry form or messaging links, the message opens in WhatsApp and is sent directly to us. We receive your name, phone number, and whatever details you choose to include. We do not operate a database or server that stores form submissions on this website.
  • Information collected automatically — like most websites, basic technical data (such as your approximate location, browser type, device, and pages visited) may be collected through cookies and similar technologies by us and by third-party services such as our advertising and analytics providers.

How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the services you request.
  • To understand how the site is used so we can improve its content and performance.
  • To display relevant advertising that helps keep this content free.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. This site, and the third-party services we use, may place cookies to remember your preferences, measure traffic, and serve advertising. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect how parts of the site function.

Advertising & Google AdSense

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google, to serve ads when you visit this website.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use our partners’ sites or apps, see Google’s Privacy & Terms.

Consent for visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we use a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) to ask for your consent before non-essential cookies — including advertising cookies — are used. You can choose to consent, decline, or manage your preferences when the consent message appears, and you can change your choice at any time.

Third-party links

This site may link to external websites (for example LinkedIn or WhatsApp). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to read their policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have shared with us, or to withdraw consent. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
